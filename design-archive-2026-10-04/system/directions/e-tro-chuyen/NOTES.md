- Idea: the app talks like a helpful family member. Every block opens with a plain Vietnamese sentence, and to-dos come as OPC message bubbles with quick-reply chips, the way people already use Zalo.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (display, sentences, huge tabular money figures; bold and friendly with Wise-like weight) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body, labels, chips; warm and very readable at 16px). Both have the Vietnamese subset.
- Colours: black ink on white, with a light #f3f4ef surface for bubbles. Wise lime #9fe870 (ink text on it) is the ONE accent, kept for money and primary actions. Status: forest green = Đã xác minh, amber = chờ, slate = Chờ mã CQT, red = Lỗi. No gold, no bank blue.
- Signature: SENTENCE-FIRST numbers. "Hôm nay nhà mình bán được 8,46 triệu, hơn hôm qua 1,34 triệu." comes before the exact 8.460.000 ₫, and the chips (Đối chiếu, Gán vào đơn) act on to-dos right inside the message.
- Self-score 7/10: the voice and figure hierarchy are clear and warm. Weakest point: the bubbles plus lime still look a bit "consumer fintech" for tax and e-invoice screens. The seriousness rule (rounded sentence, exact figure always below, status never only inside the sentence) is stated but not yet proven on a dense invoice list. The stacked bar has no legend for the amber "chờ" segment beyond the sub-line.
