- Idea: everything is a receipt (biên lai). Money and invoice items are thermal-paper slips with zigzag perforated edges sitting on a "counter" desk tone, with dotted leaders between label and figure.
- Fonts: Public Sans 400–800 for all text (sturdy and civic, full Vietnamese subset). IBM Plex Mono 500/600 only for amounts, order codes, times and bank memos, so it reads like a POS printout without mono body text.
- Colours: thermal paper #faf8f2 on desk #e4dfd3, print black #1c1b18. ONE accent, mực tím #4a3a8c (stamp-pad violet), used for the primary action, focus and the signature. Status uses separate stamp inks: ok #1d6a3a, warn #8a5200, danger #a3241b.
- Signature: the receipt slip. The slip body holds the figures and a tear-off stub holds the action. Status is a double-ruled rubber-stamp line ('ĐÃ XÁC MINH 10:43'). "Cần xử lý" is a spike, a nail driven through punched holes in the stacked slips.
- Self-score 7.5/10: the metaphor is distinctive and honest (no "all verified" stamp over money that is still pending). Weak points: the Plex Mono ₫ with its wide mono space feels loose, a lot of dashed and dotted rules could get busy on a full screen, and about 120px at the bottom of columns 2–3 is unused.
