Idea: the market price board. Ink-on-white, chunky 2px-stroked blocks, numbers you can read from a metre away; loud only where the money is.
Fonts: Big Shoulders Display 800/900 (heavy condensed, Vietnamese subset) for prices and headings, so big totals fit narrow tags. Be Vietnam Pro for body and labels (made for Vietnamese diacritics). Tabular figures throughout, and ₫ drawn at 0.6em next to display numbers.
Colours: ink #15130F, board #FFF, bg #EEECE6, plus ONE accent, vàng nghệ #F2A20C, used only as a fill behind ink text (tags, focus ring) and never as text on white. Primary buttons are solid ink. Status: ok #17703A, danger #B3261E. Pending states use a dashed ink outline, not a colour, so amber never means "warning".
Signature: the PRICE TAG (chamfered notch + string hole + 2px ink outline). Products and amounts sit on tags, and the running total is the biggest tag and the only turmeric one.
Self-score 7/10. It reads clearly and stays sober for tax. Weakest points: the ₫ glyph in Big Shoulders looks crude when small, and the tag's notch/hole may feel like a gimmick if every amount gets one. It needs a rule for when an amount gets a tag and when it is a plain figure.
