# Context for the Evaluator — OPC v7.0.0 "Onboarding: every mandatory item of a hộ kinh doanh"
OPC is a phone-first web app the Vietnamese state rolls out (Gia Lai, programme "Kế nghiệp số") to help households become hộ kinh doanh and go digital. Owner chị An is older, on a cheap Android; OPC staff can sit with her (assisted lane).
Design goal: onboarding must contain every item that is legally mandatory or needed for the end-to-end chain (research: design/_research/mandatory-2026.md, system/E2E-SCOPE.md), and show optional items with a skip. Six steps:
1 Số điện thoại + explicit data consent (Luật 91/2025) · 2 Hộ kinh doanh: has registration paper? yes → photo, OPC reads, owner checks; no → how to register at the commune (NĐ 168/2025), selling opens later · 3 Cách bán (who she sells to, goods source, hires staff → BHXH, sells food → food-safety) + "Việc OPC làm" (only the e-invoice provider must be connected; OTP/notifications are OPC's own; the rest optional with manual path) · 4 Thuế và tài khoản: VNeID level 2 + eTax (only asked; OPC never holds passwords), revenue before OPC + filing period (suggested, "chờ kế toán xác nhận"), bank accounts used for business + notified to the tax office · 5 Hoá đơn: how to issue (provider / free state e-invoice if eligible / existing), registration 01/ĐKTĐ-HĐĐT, digital signature · 6 Số đầu kỳ: products & prices, counted opening stock, cash, bank, debts (or start from 0) → done → first transaction.
Identity verification (eKYC) and bank connection are optional gates from Sẵn sàng. Sẵn sàng lists 25 items by 4 chains: mandatory first (with what each one unlocks), optional with "Bỏ qua".
Rubric: ~/.claude/skills/uiux-studio/references/evaluator-rubric.md (group mode). Pass >= 8.0, no criterion < 7. Also judge: completeness and realism for a Vietnamese hộ in 2026, whether an older owner can finish, and whether mandatory vs optional is unmistakable.
