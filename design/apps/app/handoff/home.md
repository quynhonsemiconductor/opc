## Interactions
- Pending legend item and to-do #1 both open the reconcile flow (`money.html`).
- "Vì sao tăng?" opens Trợ lý with the question pre-filled.
- `ConnectionPill`: tooltip on hover/focus (desktop), popover on tap (touch). Bar values: same.
- `/` or ⌘K focuses search / opens the command palette.

## Accessibility
- Landmarks nav · header · main. To-dos are an `<ol>`; numerals `aria-hidden` (order is semantic).
- `DayColumns`: every bar has sr-only "T7 26/9: 9.820.000 ₫". `MoneyTruthBar` bar is `role="img"` with the three amounts.
- Status is always a word + icon. Offline banner is `role="alert"`.
- Verified at text size lg/xl at 390/1024/1440 (labels wrap; nothing is cut).
