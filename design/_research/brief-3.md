# Research brief 3 — what is MANDATORY from day one (08/10/2026)

OPC = a phone-first web app the Vietnamese state (province Gia Lai, programme "Kế nghiệp số") rolls out to households so they become hộ kinh doanh and go digital, and later convert to doanh nghiệp. OPC aims at a near end-to-end ERP: setup → purchasing → stock → sales → delivery → invoice → money → books → tax filing → evidence.
Goal of this research: list precisely what is **legally mandatory** or **technically unavoidable** for (a) a hộ kinh doanh and (b) a small doanh nghiệp in Vietnam as of October 2026, and which of those need an **outside connection/integration** (and with whom), so OPC's onboarding can include every mandatory item; then what is optional but strongly recommended.

Rules: cite the legal document (number, date, effective date, article if possible) with a URL (thuvienphapluat.vn, chinhphu.vn, gdt.gov.vn, baochinhphu.vn, vbpl.vn, mof.gov.vn, reputable news). Never invent a number: write "chưa xác minh". Distinguish clearly: obligation of the household vs what software may/must do. Vietnamese, compact tables + bullets. Do not modify files.
