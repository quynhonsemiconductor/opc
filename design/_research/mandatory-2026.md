# Mandatory from day one — research result (08/10/2026)

Sources: `brief-3.md` research (3 tracks) + earlier briefs. Law changed several times in 2025–2026; every rule below is a **versioned rule set** in OPC, confirmed by an accountant before go-live, never hard-coded.

## Legal frame found (to confirm with the accountant)
| Topic | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Registering a hộ | NĐ 168/2025/NĐ-CP (30/06/2025), amended by NĐ 296/2026; forms TT 68/2025 (amended TT 121/2026). Filed at the **commune/ward** level after the 2-level reorganisation | verified by 2 sources |
| MST of a hộ | = **số định danh cá nhân of the owner** from 01/07/2025 (TT 86/2024/TT-BTC) | verified |
| Thuế khoán, lệ phí môn bài | Both **abolished from 01/01/2026** (NQ 198/2025/QH15; NĐ 362/2025) | verified |
| Tax threshold | **Conflicting**: Luật GTGT 2024 → 200 triệu; QĐ 3389/QĐ-BTC groups ≤200tr / 200tr–3 tỷ / >3 tỷ; 2026 news + NĐ 68/2026 → **1 tỷ** (households that filed Q1 above 500tr adjust and get refunds) | **must be confirmed** |
| Filing | Self-declare on **eTax / eTax Mobile**; 01/CNKD; TT 40/2021 replaced by **TT 18/2026** (05/03/2026); annual 01/TKN/CNKD | number of forms to confirm |
| E-invoice | **Luật QLT 108/2025 + NĐ 254/2026 + TT 91/2026, from 01/07/2026** (NĐ 70/2025 repealed). Hộ ≥1 tỷ selling to consumers: **HĐĐT from máy tính tiền** (no digital signature needed for that type). Other e-invoices need a digital signature. **Free e-invoices 12 months** for SMEs/HTX/hộ in difficult areas via the tax authority (NĐ 254/2026 Đ.11) | verified on several sources; details to confirm |
| E-invoice registration | Form **01/ĐKTĐ-HĐĐT**, tax authority answers in ~1 working day; identity checked against the population database (TT 91/2026 Đ.6) | partly verified |
| Bank / e-wallet | Account in the hộ's name **not required**, but **every account/e-wallet used for business must be notified** to the tax authority (from 2026) | verified in news; form & penalty to confirm |
| Books | **TT 152/2025/TT-BTC**, from 01/01/2026 (replaces TT 88/2021); books by group; keep documents 10 years (Luật Kế toán 88/2015, NĐ 174/2016) | verified; book list to confirm |
| VNeID | All eTax / public-service accounts must be **linked to VNeID level 2 by 31/12/2026**. Private apps cannot use VNeID auth unless licensed by the Ministry of Public Security (NĐ 69/2024, amended NĐ 320/2026) | verified |
| Digital signature | **VNPT SmartCA on VNeID** free for public services; commercial CA ~1–1.5 triệu/year (Viettel CA price list) | verified; whether the free one signs commercial invoices: unverified |
| Employees | Luật BHXH 41/2024 (01/07/2025): staff under labour contract → BHXH; owners of hộ enter compulsory BHXH by roadmap | verified; owner roadmap to confirm |
| Food trade | Food-safety certificate (NĐ 15/2018, NĐ 46/2026) unless exempt | to confirm for distributors |
| Customer data | Luật BVDLCN 91/2025/QH15 (01/01/2026), NĐ 356/2025 | verified |
| Hộ → doanh nghiệp | NQ 68-NQ/TW (04/05/2025); NQ 198/2025: **3-year TNDN exemption** for new SMEs (NĐ 20/2026), **free conversion advice** (NĐ 80/2021), free shared accounting platforms. A company then needs: e-invoices + digital signature always, TT 133/2016 double-entry, annual financial statements, VAT credit method, TNDN, company bank account | verified on several sources |
| Gia Lai "Kế nghiệp số" | Programme confirmed on gialai.gov.vn; decree/plan text not reachable | unverified |

## Consequence for OPC
1. OPC must also serve households **not yet registered**: onboarding branches "đã có giấy đăng ký" / "chưa có → hướng dẫn đăng ký ở xã, nhân viên OPC hỗ trợ". Selling is unlocked only after registration.
2. Nothing the state gives for free is rebuilt or charged (eTax, VNeID, SmartCA-on-VNeID, free e-invoices, abolished licence fee).
3. Thresholds and forms are data (rule set + version + accountant sign-off).
